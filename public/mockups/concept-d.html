How It Works

Get running in under an hour.

No hardware expertise required. No complex IT setup. Just install, configure, and go.

Step 1

Define your machines

Add each machine to Attraccess with its name, location, and required safety training. Upload photos and documentation so members know exactly what they're accessing.

Step 2

Assign member access

Import your member list and assign access rules per machine. Create groups like "certified welders" or "laser training complete" to manage permissions at scale.

Why Attraccess

The old way doesn't scale.

Paper sheets, key boxes, and honor systems work for 5 members. Not 50. Not 500.

❌

Before Attraccess

❌
Paper signup sheets

Lost, illegible, or simply ignored. No audit trail when accidents happen.

❌
Key hooks & shared codes

Everyone "knows" the code. No way to revoke access when a member leaves.

❌
Manual training tracking

Spreadsheets drift. Uncertified members use dangerous equipment. Liability nightmare.

❌
Zero usage visibility

Which machines are bottlenecks? Which ones haven't been used in months? No idea.

✓

With Attraccess

✓
NFC tap-in, every time

Every access event is logged with timestamp, user, and machine. Immutable, searchable.

✓
Per-user, revocable access

Revoke a member's card in one click. Access denied within seconds, everywhere.

✓
Training requirements enforced

Machine won't unlock until the member's training record is confirmed. Safety by design.

✓
Rich usage analytics

Heatmaps, peak hours, machine utilization rates. Make data-driven facility decisions.
