How it works

From unboxing to running in an afternoon.

01

Install readers on machines

Attach a small NFC reader to each machine using the provided mount. Plug in power. Takes minutes per machine.

02

Members register their NFC cards

Members link their NFC card or phone to their account via the web portal. One-time setup, no apps required.

03

Tap to access

Members tap their card on the reader. Access is granted or denied instantly. Every event is logged automatically.

04

Track & manage remotely

Manage members, permissions, and machines from the admin dashboard — from anywhere, on any device.
