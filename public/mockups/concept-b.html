NFC Access Control

Any standard NFC card — student IDs, RFID fobs, or a phone — becomes an access credential. No proprietary hardware lock-in.

  • Works with any ISO 14443-A/B NFC tag
  • Instant relay control for power outlets or solenoid locks
  • Works offline — local device handles auth, syncs when online
